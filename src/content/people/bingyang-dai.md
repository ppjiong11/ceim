Bingyang Dai works on macrophage-based therapies for musculoskeletal
regeneration, bone immunometabolism, nanoparticle-based therapy and tissue
engineering, and the crosstalk between bone and other organs.

He received his PhD from the Medical School of The Chinese University of Hong
Kong in 2020, with clinical training in orthopaedics, and holds a Certificate of
Practising Physician from the National Health Commission of China. He was a
postdoctoral research fellow at CUHK's School of Medicine from 2020 to 2024.

He serves as a Guest Associate Editor for *Bone Research* and *Frontiers in
Endocrinology*, a Guest Editor for *Bioengineering*, and a youth editorial board
member of *Extracellular Vesicles and Circulating Nucleic Acids*.
