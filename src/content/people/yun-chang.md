Yun Chang works on gene editing and directed differentiation of
human pluripotent stem cells, combined with functionalised biomaterials, for
disease immunotherapy.

He received his BS in Animal Sciences from Jilin University in 2013 and his PhD
from the Changchun Institute of Applied Chemistry, University of Chinese Academy
of Sciences, in 2019. From 2019 to 2024 he was a postdoctoral research fellow in
the Davidson School of Chemical Engineering at Purdue University. He joined the
Department of Biomedical Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University in
2024.

He has published more than 40 peer-reviewed papers, including in *Nature
Biomedical Engineering*, *Nature Communications*, *Advanced Materials*, *Cell
Reports* and *Biomaterials*, and has filed six patent applications.
