Man Ting Au works on cellular engineering for osteoarthritis treatment and drug
development, and on vascular dysfunction in age-related joint disorders. Her
research investigates endothelin signalling in the pathogenesis of
post-traumatic and post-COVID osteoarthritis.

She received her BSc in Biochemistry from The Hong Kong University of Science
and Technology and her PhD in Biomedical Engineering from PolyU in 2020, and was
a visiting researcher in the Division of Cell Matrix Biology and Regenerative
Medicine at the University of Manchester in 2018. She was a postdoctoral fellow
at PolyU from 2020 to 2024.

She received the Young Investigator Award at the 18th International Conference
on Endothelin in Rome in 2023. Her work has appeared in *Nature Microbiology*,
*Osteoarthritis and Cartilage* and *ACS Nano*.
