Cheng Dong is Chair Professor of Cell Engineering and ImmunoMedicine and
Associate Vice President (Knowledge Transfer) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic
University. He also directs the PolyU Shenzhen Research Institute.

His work spans cellular biomechanics and micro-hemodynamics, the cancer and
inflammatory microenvironment, and CAR immune cell-mediated targeted delivery
systems.

He received his BS in Engineering Mechanics and Fluid Dynamics from Shanghai
Jiao Tong University in 1982, and his MS, MPhil and PhD in Bioengineering and
Engineering Mechanics from Columbia University. He joined Penn State University
in 1992, was named Distinguished Professor of Biomedical Engineering in 2009,
and headed its Department of Biomedical Engineering from 2011 to 2022. He joined
PolyU in 2023.
