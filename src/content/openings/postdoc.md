We are looking for postdoctoral fellows across the group's range: stem cell
biology and gene editing, immunology, orthopaedic and joint biology, and
biomaterials or nanomedicine.

Relevant experience includes hPSC culture and differentiation, CRISPR-based
editing, macrophage and immune cell biology, in vivo tumour or musculoskeletal
models, and nanomaterial synthesis and characterisation. A strong publication
record and the independence to drive a project matter more than an exact match
of technique — several of our projects sit deliberately between two fields.
